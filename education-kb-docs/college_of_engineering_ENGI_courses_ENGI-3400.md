## Course Description

### Course Description

ENGI-3400 Fundamentals of Photovoltaics provides a comprehensive introduction to the science, engineering, and application of solar energy conversion through photovoltaic (PV) systems. Students will explore the physical principles underlying the photovoltaic effect, the design and characterization of solar cells, and the integration of PV components into functional energy systems. Emphasis is placed on bridging theoretical foundations with practical engineering considerations relevant to modern renewable energy development.

### Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

- Explain the semiconductor physics and quantum mechanical principles that govern the photovoltaic effect and solar cell operation
- Analyze the electrical characteristics of solar cells and PV modules, including current-voltage (I-V) curve interpretation and performance metrics
- Evaluate different PV cell technologies — including crystalline silicon, thin-film, and emerging perovskite-based cells — with respect to efficiency, cost, and application suitability
- Design and assess basic PV system configurations, including grid-tied and off-grid architectures, incorporating appropriate balance-of-system components
- Apply relevant engineering standards and energy yield estimation methods to evaluate PV system performance under varying environmental conditions

### Prerequisites

### Assessment Methods

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Midterm Examination | 20% |
| Final Examination | 30% |
| Laboratory Reports (4 sessions) | 20% |
| PV System Design Project | 20% |
| Problem Sets (weekly) | 10% |

**Midterm and Final Examinations** assess theoretical knowledge of semiconductor physics, cell characterization, and system design principles through a combination of short-answer and quantitative problem-solving questions.

**Laboratory Reports** document findings from hands-on sessions in which students measure and analyze the performance characteristics of physical PV modules under controlled and variable conditions.

**PV System Design Project** is a semester-long team-based assignment in which student groups propose, model, and present a complete PV installation for a hypothetical facility, including energy yield projections, component selection, and economic analysis.

**Problem Sets** are assigned weekly and cover core computational and conceptual topics from lecture material.
