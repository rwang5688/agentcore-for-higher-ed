## Course Description

### Course Description

ENGI-1600: Dynamics and Control I provides a rigorous introduction to the principles governing the motion of mechanical systems and the foundational concepts of automatic control theory. Students will explore Newtonian and Lagrangian mechanics, system modelling, and the mathematical tools used to analyse and design feedback control systems. This course serves as a critical gateway to advanced study in mechanical, electrical, and systems engineering, bridging theoretical dynamics with practical control applications.

---

### Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

- Apply Newtonian and Lagrangian formulations to model and analyse the dynamics of rigid body and multi-body mechanical systems
- Derive and interpret mathematical models of physical systems using differential equations and transfer function representations
- Analyse the stability, transient response, and steady-state behaviour of linear time-invariant (LTI) control systems
- Design and evaluate basic feedback control strategies, including proportional, integral, and derivative (PID) controllers, to meet specified performance criteria
- Utilise computational tools and simulation environments to model, simulate, and validate dynamic and control system behaviour

---

### Prerequisites

Students must have successfully completed the following prior to enrolment:

- **ENGI-2210: Engineering Mathematics III** – covering ordinary differential equations, Laplace transforms, and linear algebra
- **ENGI-2340: Mechanics of Solids and Systems** – providing foundational knowledge of statics, kinematics, and introductory dynamics
- **ENGI-2450: Signals and Systems** – introducing frequency-domain analysis and system representation
- A minimum cumulative GPA of 2.5 in all prior Engineering coursework is recommended

|---|---|---|

| Final Examination | 30% | Comprehensive written examination encompassing all course content, including control system design and stability analysis |

### Required Materials and Textbooks

Students are expected to obtain the following materials prior to the first week of classes:

- **Kasprowicz, D. & Feld, M. R.** (4th ed.). *Engineering Dynamics: Principles, Methods, and Applications*. Meridian Technical Press. — Primary text covering Newtonian and Lagrangian dynamics, rigid body motion, and vibrations.

- **Ohlendorf, S. J., Tarrant, C. & Mbeki, P.** (2nd ed.). *Feedback Control Systems: Analysis and Design for Engineers*. Crestline Academic Publishing. — Core reference for control theory, transfer functions, root locus, frequency response, and PID design.

- **Wittenberg, L.** (1st ed.). *Computational Modelling in Dynamics and Control: A MATLAB and Simulink Approach*. Northgate Engineering Texts. — Supplementary computational reference used throughout laboratory sessions and the design project.

- Access to **MATLAB/Simulink** (current institutional licence) is required; software is available through the College of Engineering computing laboratories or via the university's student software portal at no additional cost.

- A bound engineering calculation notebook is required for laboratory sessions; loose-leaf submissions will not be accepted.
