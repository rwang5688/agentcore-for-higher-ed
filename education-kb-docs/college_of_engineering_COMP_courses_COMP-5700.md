## Course Description

### Course Description

COMP-5700: Deep Learning provides an advanced examination of neural network architectures and their application to complex computational problems, including computer vision, natural language processing, and sequential data modeling. Students will develop both the theoretical foundations and practical implementation skills necessary to design, train, and evaluate deep learning systems using contemporary frameworks. Emphasis is placed on understanding the mathematical underpinnings of modern deep learning methods alongside hands-on experimentation with large-scale datasets and real-world applications.

### Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

- Design and implement a range of deep neural network architectures, including convolutional neural networks (CNNs), recurrent neural networks (RNNs), transformers, and generative models, selecting appropriate structures for given problem domains
- Analyze the theoretical principles governing deep learning, including optimization landscapes, backpropagation, regularization techniques, and the challenges of training deep models at scale
- Apply modern deep learning frameworks (such as PyTorch or TensorFlow) to develop end-to-end pipelines for data preprocessing, model training, hyperparameter tuning, and performance evaluation
- Critically evaluate published deep learning research, identifying methodological strengths and limitations, and situating experimental results within the broader literature
- Formulate and execute an original deep learning research or application project, demonstrating competency in problem scoping, experimental design, and results interpretation

### Prerequisites

Students must have successfully completed the following before enrolling in COMP-5700:

- **COMP-3410: Machine Learning** – foundational understanding of supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning paradigms
- **COMP-3220: Algorithms and Data Structures** – proficiency in algorithmic thinking and computational complexity analysis
- **MATH-2310: Linear Algebra** – familiarity with matrix operations, eigendecomposition, and vector spaces essential for understanding neural network operations
- **MATH-2450: Probability and Statistics** – working knowledge of probability distributions, statistical inference, and stochastic processes
- **COMP-2150: Introduction to Python Programming** or demonstrated programming proficiency in a relevant high-level language

Students lacking one or more prerequisites may seek instructor permission by submitting a petition to the Department of Computer Science no later than one week prior to the start of the semester.

### Assessment Methods

Student performance in COMP-5700 will be evaluated through the following components:

- **Programming Assignments (30%)** – A series of four structured assignments requiring students to implement and experiment with neural network architectures from foundational feedforward networks through to attention-based models. Assignments are completed individually and assessed on both correctness and code quality.
- **Midterm Examination (15%)** – A written in-class examination covering theoretical concepts, mathematical derivations, and conceptual analysis of deep learning methods addressed in the first half of the course.
- **Research Paper Reviews (10%)** – Students will submit three critical written reviews of assigned papers from the deep learning literature, each approximately 500–700 words, demonstrating the ability to evaluate methodological rigor and situate contributions within the field.
- **Group Research Project (35%)** – Teams of two to three students will identify an original problem, develop a deep learning solution, and produce a final written report (minimum 8 pages in conference paper format) along with an oral presentation delivered during the final two weeks of the semester. Projects are evaluated on novelty, technical soundness, quality of experimentation, and clarity of communication.
- **Final Examination (10%)** – A comprehensive written examination administered during the university's official final examination period, emphasizing integration of course topics and critical analysis of deep learning systems.
