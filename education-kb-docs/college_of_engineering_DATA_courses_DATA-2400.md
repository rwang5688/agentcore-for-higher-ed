## Course Description

### Course Description

This course provides students with a foundational understanding of big data concepts, technologies, and analytical frameworks used in modern data-driven environments. Students will explore the defining characteristics of big data—volume, velocity, variety, veracity, and value—alongside the tools and platforms used to store, process, and derive insight from large-scale datasets. Emphasis is placed on practical application, ethical considerations, and the role of big data in shaping contemporary decision-making across industry and research.

### Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of DATA-2400, students will be able to:

- Define and explain the core properties of big data and articulate how they differ from traditional data management paradigms
- Identify and describe commonly used big data technologies and distributed computing frameworks, including storage systems and processing pipelines
- Apply introductory data wrangling and querying techniques to large, heterogeneous datasets using appropriate tools
- Evaluate the ethical, legal, and privacy implications of collecting, storing, and analysing big data at scale
- Interpret and communicate analytical findings derived from large datasets in a clear and meaningful way for both technical and non-technical audiences

### Prerequisites

Students enrolling in DATA-2400 must have successfully completed the following, or demonstrate equivalent competency:

- **DATA-1100: Introduction to Data Science** or **CS-1150: Foundations of Computing**
- **MATH-1210: Introduction to Statistics and Probability**
- A minimum passing grade of C or above in all prerequisite courses is required

Students without formal computing background are encouraged to consult with their academic advisor before registering.

### Assessment Methods

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Midterm Examination | 20% |
| Final Examination | 25% |
| Laboratory Assignments (×4) | 25% |
| Group Data Analysis Project | 20% |
| Participation & Weekly Reflections | 10% |

**Midterm and Final Examinations** assess conceptual understanding of big data principles, architectures, and ethical frameworks through a combination of multiple-choice, short-answer, and scenario-based questions.

**Laboratory Assignments** are hands-on exercises completed individually, requiring students to work with real-world datasets using course-approved tools. Assignments are submitted via the course portal and graded on both technical accuracy and written interpretation.

**Group Data Analysis Project** tasks teams of three to four students with selecting a publicly available large dataset, applying course methodologies to process and analyse it, and presenting findings in a written report and a final in-class presentation. Project topics must be approved by the instructional team by Week 5.

**Participation & Weekly Reflections** consist of short written responses to assigned readings and in-class discussion contributions, evaluated on quality of engagement rather than quantity.
