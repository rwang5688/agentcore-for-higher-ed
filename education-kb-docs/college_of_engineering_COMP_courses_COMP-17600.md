## Course Description

### Course Description

COMP-17600: Understanding Computers and the Internet provides students with a foundational survey of modern computing systems, digital infrastructure, and the principles underlying the global internet. Students will explore how hardware and software interact, how data is transmitted across networks, and how everyday digital tools and services function at a conceptual and technical level. This course is designed for students across all disciplines seeking a rigorous and accessible entry point into computer science and digital literacy.

### Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

- Identify and describe the core components of a computer system, including hardware architecture, operating systems, and software layers
- Explain the fundamental principles of how the internet operates, including networking protocols, data transmission, and the client-server model
- Evaluate issues related to cybersecurity, digital privacy, and the ethical responsibilities of individuals and institutions in online environments
- Demonstrate basic proficiency in navigating and critically assessing digital tools, web technologies, and cloud-based services
- Apply conceptual computing knowledge to analyse real-world scenarios involving technology, data, and digital communication

### Prerequisites

There are no formal prerequisites for COMP-17600. Students are expected to possess general computer literacy at the level of routine personal or academic use (e.g., word processing, email, and web browsing). Students who have not previously engaged with academic computing environments are encouraged to review available introductory digital skills resources through the Peculiar U Library and Learning Commons prior to the first week of instruction.

### Required Materials

Students are expected to obtain the following materials prior to the first scheduled class meeting:

- **Harwick, P. J., & Olenfeld, S. T.** (2021). *Foundations of Digital Systems: Computers, Networks, and the Modern Web* (4th ed.). Crestline Academic Press. ISBN: 978-1-55032-841-7
- **Dressel, M., & Yuen, C.** (2019). *The Connected World: An Introduction to Internet Architecture and Society* (2nd ed.). Northgate University Publishing. ISBN: 978-0-47291-306-2

A course packet containing supplementary readings, case studies, and lab instruction sheets will be made available through the Peculiar U course management portal (PeculiarLearn) at no additional cost. Students will also require access to a personal computer with a standard web browser and a stable internet connection for online lab components. All required software for laboratory assignments is freely available and installation instructions will be provided during the first week of class.
