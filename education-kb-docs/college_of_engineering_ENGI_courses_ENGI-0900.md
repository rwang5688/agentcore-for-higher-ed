### Course Description

This advanced course provides a comprehensive examination of the principles, architectures, and analytical frameworks underlying modern communications systems, with emphasis on signal transmission, modulation techniques, noise analysis, and channel capacity. Students will engage with both analog and digital communication paradigms, exploring real-world system design challenges across wired, wireless, and optical media. The course integrates theoretical foundations with applied engineering practice, preparing students to design, evaluate, and optimise communications systems for professional deployment.

---

### Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

- Analyse and apply core principles of signal modulation and demodulation, including AM, FM, PSK, and QAM schemes, in the context of practical communications system design
- Evaluate the performance of communications systems under conditions of noise, interference, and bandwidth constraint using Shannon's theorem and related information-theoretic tools
- Design and assess digital transmission systems, incorporating error detection and correction coding strategies appropriate to specified channel conditions
- Demonstrate proficiency in the use of computational modelling tools to simulate and validate communications system behaviour prior to physical implementation
- Critically appraise emerging developments in communications technology — including software-defined radio, 5G architectures, and optical networking — and assess their implications for engineering practice

### Assessment Methods

| Component | Weight | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Midterm Examination | 20% | Closed-book written examination covering signal theory, modulation, and noise analysis (Weeks 1–7) |
| Laboratory Portfolio | 25% | Four structured laboratory sessions requiring written technical reports; assessed on methodology, data interpretation, and clarity of analysis |
| Systems Design Project | 30% | Individual or paired project requiring the end-to-end design, simulation, and documentation of a functional communications system; formal written report and oral defence required |
| Final Examination | 25% | Comprehensive closed-book examination covering all course content; includes both analytical problem-solving and short-answer conceptual questions |

Students must achieve a passing grade in both the Final Examination and the Systems Design Project independently in order to pass the course overall. Laboratory attendance is compulsory; more than one unexcused absence may result in failure of the Laboratory Portfolio component.
