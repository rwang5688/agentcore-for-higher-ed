## Course Description

**DATA-1800 | Data Science: Wrangling**
**Level 635 | 3 Credit Points | College of Engineering — Department of Data Science**

---

This course provides an advanced examination of data wrangling methodologies, equipping students with the theoretical foundations and applied skills necessary to acquire, clean, reshape, and prepare complex, real-world datasets for analytical pipelines. Students will engage critically with the challenges inherent in messy, incomplete, and heterogeneous data sources across a variety of domains and formats. Emphasis is placed on reproducible, scalable workflows and industry-standard tooling appropriate for professional and research-oriented data science practice.

---

### Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

- Demonstrate proficiency in programmatic data acquisition from structured, semi-structured, and unstructured sources, including APIs, relational databases, and flat files
- Apply systematic techniques for detecting and resolving data quality issues, including missing values, duplicate records, inconsistent encodings, and outliers
- Design and implement reproducible data transformation pipelines using modern data manipulation frameworks and version-controlled workflows
- Evaluate and select appropriate data reshaping and integration strategies — including joins, aggregations, and pivoting operations — given the constraints of a target analytical task
- Critically assess the ethical implications of data collection, transformation decisions, and representation choices, particularly with respect to bias introduced during the wrangling process

### Prerequisites

Students must have successfully completed the following prior to enrollment:

### Assessment Methods

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Weekly Data Wrangling Lab Exercises | 20% |
| Midterm Examination | 20% |
| Reproducible Pipeline Project (Phase I & II) | 30% |
| Final Comprehensive Examination | 20% |
| Participation & In-Class Critiques | 10% |

**Weekly Lab Exercises:** Short, applied assignments submitted through the course platform each week, requiring students to wrangle provided or self-sourced datasets according to specified analytical goals.

**Midterm Examination:** A closed-book written and practical examination assessing theoretical understanding of data quality concepts, transformation logic, and pipeline design.

**Reproducible Pipeline Project:** A two-phase semester project in which students identify a domain dataset of meaningful complexity, document its provenance and quality issues, and develop a fully reproducible wrangling pipeline accompanied by a written technical report. Phase I addresses acquisition and profiling; Phase II addresses transformation and validation.

**Final Examination:** A comprehensive closed-book examination covering all course material, including both conceptual and applied problem-solving components.
