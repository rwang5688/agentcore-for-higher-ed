### Course Description

This course provides a rigorous introduction to the principles and applications of electronic feedback systems, with emphasis on the analysis and design of closed-loop control circuits in analog and digital contexts. Students will examine how feedback mechanisms are employed to regulate stability, reduce distortion, and improve the performance of amplifiers, oscillators, and other electronic systems. Practical laboratory work complements theoretical instruction, equipping students to evaluate and construct functional feedback circuits in real-world engineering scenarios.

---

### Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

- Explain the fundamental principles of negative and positive feedback and their respective effects on gain, bandwidth, and system stability in electronic circuits
- Apply loop gain analysis techniques, including Bode plots and Nyquist criteria, to assess and predict the stability of feedback systems
- Design and analyze common feedback amplifier topologies — including series-series, series-shunt, shunt-series, and shunt-shunt configurations — and evaluate their input/output impedance characteristics
- Simulate and test electronic feedback circuits using industry-standard software tools, interpreting simulation output against theoretical predictions
- Diagnose instability, oscillation, and performance degradation in feedback systems and propose appropriate corrective design modifications
