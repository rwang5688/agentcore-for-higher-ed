### Course Description

This course provides a comprehensive examination of linear integrated circuits, with particular emphasis on the analysis, design, and application of operational amplifiers and related analog circuit components. Students will investigate the fundamental characteristics of linear ICs in both open-loop and closed-loop configurations, exploring their roles in signal conditioning, filtering, amplification, and waveform generation. Practical laboratory work reinforces theoretical concepts, equipping students with the technical proficiency required for professional analog circuit design and evaluation.

---

### Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

- Analyse the internal architecture and terminal characteristics of operational amplifiers, including ideal and non-ideal behaviours such as offset voltage, bias currents, slew rate, and bandwidth limitations
- Design and evaluate linear amplifier circuits — including inverting, non-inverting, summing, and differential configurations — to meet specified gain and impedance requirements
- Apply linear integrated circuits to practical signal-processing tasks, including the construction of active filters, integrators, differentiators, voltage regulators, and instrumentation amplifiers
- Interpret manufacturer datasheets and apply industry-standard specifications to the selection and implementation of appropriate linear IC components for real-world engineering scenarios
- Conduct experimental laboratory investigations, accurately measure circuit performance parameters, and produce rigorous technical documentation in the form of structured laboratory reports
