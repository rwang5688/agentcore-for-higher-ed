### Course Description

This course provides students with a rigorous foundation in the principles and practices of managing end-to-end data analysis workflows within organizational and technical contexts. Students will explore strategies for structuring, overseeing, and communicating data analysis processes, with emphasis on reproducibility, quality assurance, and collaborative project management. The course bridges technical data skills with practical managerial competencies, preparing students to lead data-driven initiatives in professional environments.

### Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

- Design and implement structured data analysis pipelines that prioritize reproducibility, transparency, and documentation best practices
- Apply project management frameworks and methodologies to coordinate multi-stage data analysis efforts within team-based settings
- Evaluate data quality throughout the analysis lifecycle, identifying sources of error, bias, and inconsistency and applying appropriate remediation strategies
- Communicate analytical findings effectively to both technical and non-technical stakeholders through written reports, visualizations, and oral presentations
- Critically assess ethical considerations in data collection, management, and analysis, including issues of privacy, consent, and algorithmic accountability

### Prerequisites

Students enrolling in DATA-3100 must have successfully completed the following:

- **DATA-2200: Foundations of Data Science** or equivalent, with a minimum grade of C+
- **DATA-2310: Introduction to Statistical Computing**
- **MATH-2150: Applied Statistics and Probability**

Students without formal prerequisites who possess equivalent demonstrated experience may petition the Department of Data Science for a prerequisite waiver. Approval is at the discretion of the course coordinator.

### Assessment Methods

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Midterm Examination | 20% |
| Final Examination | 25% |
| Data Analysis Project (Group) | 30% |
| Weekly Lab Assignments (x8) | 15% |
| Reflective Portfolio | 10% |

**Midterm and Final Examinations** assess theoretical knowledge of analysis frameworks, workflow management, and data quality concepts. **The Group Data Analysis Project** requires teams of three to four students to manage and execute a complete data analysis workflow from problem scoping through to stakeholder presentation, with individual contribution assessments included. **Weekly Lab Assignments** provide structured, hands-on practice with tools and techniques introduced in lectures. The **Reflective Portfolio** asks students to document their development as analytical practitioners across the semester, culminating in a final reflective essay submitted during the examination period.

### Required Materials

Students are expected to obtain the following materials prior to the first week of instruction:

- Hartwell, S. G., & Pomeranz, D. (2021). *Governing the Pipeline: A Practical Guide to Data Analysis Management* (3rd ed.). Halcyon Academic Press.
- Vásquez-Ortiz, C. (2019). *Reproducible Analysis in Practice: Workflows, Documentation, and Collaboration*. Northbridge Publishing.
- Dalloway, T. R., & Einhorn, L. (2022). *Quality and Accountability in Data-Driven Organizations*. Meridian Textbooks.

Students will also require access to the university-licensed **AnalytiCore** software suite, available at no cost through the College of Engineering software portal. Supplementary readings will be distributed via the course management system throughout the semester.
