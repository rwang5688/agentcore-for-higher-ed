## Course Description

### Course Description

ENGI-3600: Green Supply Chain Management provides a comprehensive examination of sustainable practices, environmental strategies, and systems thinking as applied to the design and management of modern supply chains. Students will explore the integration of ecological considerations into procurement, logistics, manufacturing, and distribution processes, with an emphasis on reducing environmental impact while maintaining operational efficiency. The course draws on engineering principles, lifecycle analysis, and policy frameworks to equip students with the tools necessary to address real-world sustainability challenges within global supply networks.

### Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

- Analyze the environmental impact of supply chain operations and identify opportunities for reducing carbon footprints, waste generation, and resource consumption across the full product lifecycle
- Apply systems thinking and engineering principles to design or redesign supply chain processes that align with green and circular economy frameworks
- Evaluate procurement and logistics strategies using sustainability metrics, environmental management standards (including ISO 14001), and regulatory compliance requirements
- Develop and assess green supplier selection criteria, incorporating environmental performance indicators alongside traditional cost and quality benchmarks
- Critically engage with case studies and emerging technologies—such as reverse logistics, industrial symbiosis, and green transportation—to formulate evidence-based recommendations for sustainable supply chain improvement

### Assessment Methods

Student performance in ENGI-3600 will be evaluated through a combination of the following assessment components:

- **Midterm Examination (20%):** A written examination covering foundational concepts in green supply chain theory, environmental standards, and lifecycle assessment methodologies, administered in-class during Week 8.
- **Final Examination (25%):** A comprehensive written examination assessing students' integrated understanding of all course material, including quantitative problem-solving and conceptual analysis of sustainability trade-offs in supply chain design.
- **Green Supply Chain Audit Project (30%):** Working in groups of three to four, students will conduct a detailed environmental audit of a real or simulated company's supply chain. Teams will submit a written report and deliver a formal 20-minute presentation to the class. The project requires identification of inefficiencies, application of sustainability frameworks, and the proposal of actionable improvement strategies.
- **Case Study Analyses (15%):** Students will complete three individual written case study analyses throughout the semester, each examining a distinct sector or geographic context in which green supply chain practices have been implemented or are needed. Each submission should be approximately 800–1,000 words.
- **Participation and Weekly Reflections (10%):** Students are expected to engage meaningfully in seminar discussions and submit brief weekly reflection entries (150–200 words) responding to assigned readings or guest lecture content.
