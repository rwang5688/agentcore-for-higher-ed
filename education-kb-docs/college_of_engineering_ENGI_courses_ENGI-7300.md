## Course Description

### Course Description

ENGI-7300 provides an advanced examination of the principles, design, and applications of laser systems and fiber optic technologies within modern engineering contexts. Students will explore the physics of light amplification, coherent emission, and guided wave propagation, bridging theoretical foundations with practical implementation in communication, sensing, and industrial systems. This course emphasizes current industry standards and emerging research directions, preparing students for professional and academic work at the intersection of photonics and optical engineering.

---

### Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

- Explain the fundamental quantum mechanical and electromagnetic principles underlying laser operation, including stimulated emission, population inversion, and optical resonator design
- Analyze the propagation characteristics of optical signals within single-mode and multi-mode fiber optic systems, including attenuation, dispersion, and bandwidth limitations
- Evaluate and select appropriate laser and fiber optic components for specific engineering applications such as telecommunications, medical instrumentation, and precision manufacturing
- Design basic fiber optic transmission links and perform quantitative system budgeting, including loss calculations and signal integrity analysis
- Critically assess current and emerging developments in photonic integration, high-power laser systems, and advanced fiber sensor technologies through engagement with primary research literature

| Midterm Examination | 20% | Closed-book written examination covering laser physics, optical resonator theory, and foundational fiber optic principles |
