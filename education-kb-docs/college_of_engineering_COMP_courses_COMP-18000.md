## Course Description

### Course Description

This course provides a comprehensive introduction to the design, implementation, and management of Wide Area Networks (WANs), examining the protocols, technologies, and architectures that enable data communication across large geographic distances. Students will explore fundamental WAN concepts including transmission media, routing protocols, and service provider infrastructure, with an emphasis on real-world application and network troubleshooting. The course combines theoretical frameworks with practical laboratory exercises to develop foundational competencies in network administration and configuration.

### Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

- Identify and describe the core components and topologies that comprise Wide Area Network infrastructure, including leased lines, circuit-switched, and packet-switched networks
- Analyze and apply common WAN protocols such as HDLC, PPP, Frame Relay, and MPLS within appropriate networking contexts
- Configure and troubleshoot basic WAN connections using industry-standard simulation and laboratory tools
- Evaluate the performance characteristics and security considerations of various WAN technologies and service models
- Demonstrate an understanding of emerging WAN technologies, including SD-WAN and cloud-based networking solutions, and their implications for modern enterprise environments

### Assessment Methods

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Midterm Examination | 20% |
| Final Examination | 25% |
| Laboratory Assignments (6 sessions) | 25% |
| Network Design Project | 20% |
| Participation & In-Class Quizzes | 10% |

**Midterm and Final Examinations:** Written examinations assessing theoretical knowledge of WAN protocols, architectures, and technologies. The final examination is comprehensive and includes both multiple-choice and short-answer components.

**Laboratory Assignments:** Six structured lab sessions conducted using network simulation software, in which students configure and test WAN connections in controlled virtual environments. Lab reports are submitted following each session.

**Network Design Project:** A semester-long capstone project in which students design a WAN solution for a provided enterprise scenario. The project is submitted in two stages — a design proposal (Week 9) and a final report with documentation (Week 14) — and may be completed individually or in pairs.

**Participation & In-Class Quizzes:** Short unannounced quizzes administered periodically throughout the semester, alongside evaluated contribution to class discussions and group problem-solving activities.
