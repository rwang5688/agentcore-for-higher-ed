## Course Description

### Course Description

ENVI-2800 provides a comprehensive introduction to radar systems and their applications within environmental and natural resource monitoring contexts. Students will explore the fundamental principles of electromagnetic wave propagation, signal transmission and reception, and the interpretation of radar-derived data for environmental analysis. The course bridges theoretical foundations with applied practice, equipping students with the technical literacy necessary to utilize radar technology in fields such as meteorology, land surface monitoring, hydrology, and ecological assessment.

### Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

- Explain the physical principles underlying radar operation, including electromagnetic wave behaviour, pulse transmission, backscatter, and signal processing fundamentals
- Identify and distinguish between major radar system types — including synthetic aperture radar (SAR), Doppler radar, and ground-penetrating radar — and evaluate their respective applications in environmental monitoring
- Interpret and critically analyse radar imagery and datasets to extract meaningful environmental information relating to land cover, weather systems, soil moisture, and vegetation structure
- Apply basic quantitative methods to assess radar signal returns and relate these measurements to real-world environmental variables and phenomena
- Evaluate the limitations, error sources, and ethical considerations associated with radar-based remote sensing in natural resource management and environmental decision-making

### Prerequisites

Students enrolling in ENVI-2800 must have successfully completed the following:

- **ENVI-1100: Introduction to Environmental Remote Sensing** or equivalent
- **PHYS-1200: Fundamentals of Physics II** (covering waves, optics, and electromagnetism)
- **MATH-1300: Calculus and Introductory Statistics for Environmental Sciences**

Students without these prerequisites but with demonstrable equivalent experience may seek instructor consent to enrol. A minimum cumulative GPA of 2.0 is recommended.

### Required Materials

Students are required to obtain the following texts prior to the first week of instruction:

- Halverson, T. R., & Joensen, C. P. (2019). *Radar Systems and Environmental Sensing: Principles and Practice* (3rd ed.). Northgate Academic Press.
- Delfino, A. & Strachan, W. O. (2021). *Applied Synthetic Aperture Radar for Earth Observation* (2nd ed.). Meridian Technical Publishers.
- Wulfsberg, I. (2018). *Signal Interpretation and Geophysical Remote Sensing: A Quantitative Approach*. Crescent Hill University Press.

A supplementary course pack compiled by the instructional team — including selected journal articles, technical documentation, and laboratory exercise materials — will be made available through the Peculiar U Learning Management System at no additional cost. Students will require access to **RadarScope Pro** (educational licence available through the university) and the open-source **SNAP Toolbox** for laboratory components.
