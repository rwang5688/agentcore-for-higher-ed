## Course Description

### Course Description

ENGI-1700: Electrical provides a rigorous examination of fundamental and advanced electrical engineering principles, with emphasis on circuit analysis, electromagnetic theory, and systems design. Students will engage with both theoretical frameworks and applied methodologies to develop competencies in analysing, designing, and evaluating complex electrical systems. This course bridges core engineering concepts with contemporary industry applications, preparing students for advanced specialisation within the discipline.

### Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

- Analyse and solve complex electrical circuits using advanced techniques including Thevenin and Norton equivalent circuit methods, superposition, and Laplace transform analysis
- Apply electromagnetic field theory to the design and evaluation of electrical components and systems
- Design and critically assess electrical systems in accordance with established safety standards and engineering ethics
- Demonstrate proficiency in the use of simulation and modelling tools for the testing and optimisation of electrical systems
- Synthesise theoretical knowledge and practical skills to produce comprehensive technical reports and design solutions appropriate to industry standards

### Prerequisites

Students must have completed the following prior to enrolment:

- ENGI-1400: Circuit Theory and Analysis (or equivalent)
- ENGI-1450: Applied Mathematics for Engineers II
- PHYS-1300: Electromagnetism and Wave Physics
- A minimum cumulative GPA of 2.5 in all prior Engineering coursework

Students lacking one or more prerequisites may seek written permission from the course coordinator.

### Assessment Methods

| Assessment Component | Weighting | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Midterm Examination | 25% | Written closed-book examination covering circuit analysis and electromagnetic theory |
| Final Examination | 30% | Comprehensive closed-book examination spanning all course content |
| Design Project | 25% | Group-based electrical systems design project with written report and oral presentation |
| Laboratory Reports | 15% | Four graded laboratory reports submitted throughout the semester |
| Tutorial Participation | 5% | Active engagement in weekly tutorial and problem-solving sessions |

Students must achieve a passing grade in both the final examination and the design project to pass the course overall. Late submissions will be penalised at 10% per business day unless an extension has been formally approved.

### Required Materials

The following texts are required for this course and should be obtained prior to the first week of instruction:

- Vanthorpe, R. L. & Osei, M. J. (2019). *Principles of Electrical Engineering: Analysis, Design, and Application* (4th ed.). Bridgemont Academic Press.
- Falkenrath, D. H. (2021). *Electromagnetic Systems: Theory and Modern Practice* (2nd ed.). Harwick Engineering Publishers.
- Nalluri, S. & Czernowski, P. (2020). *Advanced Circuit Analysis: A Systematic Approach*. Ellenford Technical Press.

Students are also advised to obtain access to **CircuitSim Pro** (Version 8.2 or later), the simulation software used throughout laboratory and design components of this course. A licensed version is available through the College of Engineering student software portal at no additional cost.
