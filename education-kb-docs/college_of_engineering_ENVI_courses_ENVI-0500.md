### Course Description

This course provides a rigorous and comprehensive introduction to the physical, chemical, biological, and geological dimensions of the world's ocean systems, equipping students with the foundational scientific literacy required for advanced study in environmental and marine sciences. Students will examine ocean circulation, marine ecosystems, seafloor dynamics, and the ocean's critical role in regulating Earth's climate, with particular attention to how anthropogenic pressures are reshaping these systems in the contemporary era. Drawing on interdisciplinary methodologies and current field data, the course bridges theoretical frameworks with real-world applications relevant to environmental engineering, resource management, and coastal policy.

---

### Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of ENVI-0500, students will be able to:

- Describe and analyse the major physical and chemical properties of seawater, including thermohaline circulation, ocean stratification, and biogeochemical cycling, and explain their significance for global climate regulation.
- Evaluate the geological processes underlying ocean basin formation, seafloor spreading, and sediment dynamics, situating these phenomena within the broader context of plate tectonics.
- Identify and characterise the primary marine ecological zones and biomes, demonstrating an understanding of biodiversity patterns, trophic structures, and ecosystem services provided by ocean environments.
- Apply quantitative and spatial analytical tools — including oceanographic datasets, remote sensing imagery, and hydrographic modelling software — to interpret and communicate observed patterns in ocean systems.
- Critically assess the impact of climate change, ocean acidification, overfishing, and pollution on marine systems, and evaluate the scientific basis for current and proposed conservation and management strategies.

### Prerequisites

Students enrolling in ENVI-0500 should have successfully completed the following or demonstrate equivalent competency:

- **ENVI-0300: Earth Systems and Environmental Science** or an approved equivalent introductory earth or environmental science course
- **CHEM-0210: General Chemistry II** or equivalent, including a working knowledge of aqueous chemistry and acid-base equilibria
- **MATH-0220: Calculus and Statistics for Science** or equivalent, given the quantitative demands of oceanographic data analysis at the 500 level

Students with strong backgrounds in physical geography, geology, or biological sciences are encouraged to consult the course instructors regarding enrolment eligibility. Prior laboratory experience is strongly recommended.

### Assessment Methods

Assessment in ENVI-0500 is designed to evaluate both conceptual mastery and the practical application of oceanographic knowledge across a variety of formats.

| Assessment Component | Weight | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Midterm Examination | 20% | A written examination combining multiple-choice and short-answer questions covering Weeks 1–7 material |
| Final Examination | 25% | A comprehensive written examination including short-answer and extended-response questions |
| Laboratory Reports (×4) | 20% | Four structured reports submitted following designated laboratory sessions; assessed on data interpretation, methodology, and scientific communication |
| Research Paper | 20% | A 3,500–4,500 word individually authored paper examining a contemporary issue in ocean science (e.g., deep-sea mining, marine heatwaves, plastic accumulation zones); topic proposals due Week 4 |
| Group Fieldwork Project | 10% | A collaborative investigation using local aquatic or coastal data, culminating in a group presentation delivered in Week 12 |
| Participation & In-Class Exercises | 5% | Active engagement in seminars, discussion boards, and in-class analytical exercises |

*Note: All written assessments must adhere to Peculiar U's Academic Integrity Policy. Late submissions will be penalised at 5% per business day unless prior arrangements have been made with the course coordinator.*
