### Course Description

This advanced course equips students with the theoretical foundations and practical skills necessary to design, build, and deploy production-ready data products across a range of real-world contexts. Students explore the full lifecycle of a data product — from ideation and prototyping through to deployment, monitoring, and iterative refinement — with emphasis on scalability, usability, and ethical responsibility. Special attention is given to the intersection of engineering rigour and user-centred design, preparing students to deliver data-driven solutions that generate measurable value within organisational and societal settings.

---

### Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

- Design and architect end-to-end data product pipelines that integrate data ingestion, transformation, modelling, and delivery components in a coherent and maintainable framework.
- Apply software engineering best practices — including version control, containerisation, and continuous integration — to the development and deployment of robust, production-grade data products.
- Evaluate and select appropriate technologies, platforms, and APIs to support interactive data applications, dashboards, and automated reporting systems.
- Critically assess the ethical, privacy, and governance considerations inherent in deploying data products to end users, and implement appropriate safeguards and transparency mechanisms.
- Communicate technical design decisions and product outcomes effectively to both technical and non-technical stakeholders through documentation, demonstrations, and written reports.

### Prerequisites

Students enrolling in DATA-1900 are expected to have successfully completed the following or demonstrated equivalent competency:

- **DATA-1640: Applied Machine Learning Systems** or equivalent graduate-level machine learning coursework
- **DATA-1580: Data Engineering and Pipeline Architecture**
- **COMP-1420: Cloud Computing and Distributed Systems** or documented professional experience with cloud deployment environments
- Proficiency in Python or R at an advanced level, including familiarity with relevant data science libraries and frameworks (e.g., Pandas, Scikit-learn, Tidyverse)

Students who are uncertain whether their background meets these requirements are encouraged to consult with the course coordinators prior to enrolment.

### Assessment Methods

| Assessment Component | Weighting | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Data Product Capstone Project | 45% | Teams of 3–4 students will design, develop, and deploy a fully functional data product addressing a defined problem domain. Deliverables include a working application, technical documentation, and a live demonstration presented to a panel of instructors and invited industry reviewers. |
| Technical Design Paper | 25% | An individual written paper (4,000–5,000 words) critically analysing the architectural and ethical decisions made during the development of the team capstone project, with reference to relevant literature and industry standards. |
| Midterm Examination | 15% | A closed-book written examination assessing students' understanding of data product lifecycle frameworks, deployment architectures, and governance principles covered in the first half of the course. |
| Weekly Lab Assignments | 15% | A series of structured laboratory exercises completed individually, designed to build incrementally toward the skills required for the capstone project. Assessed on correctness, code quality, and reflective commentary. |

*A passing grade of at least 50% on the Capstone Project is required to pass the course overall, regardless of aggregate score.*
